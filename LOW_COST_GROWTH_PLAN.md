# Low-cost growth plan

## Zero-cost weekly actions

- Publish one SEO article per week from the Focus Files format.
- Post one LinkedIn carousel per week: one ADHD struggle, one strategy, one CTA.
- Turn each strategy card into a short post.
- Add one free printable resource per month.
- Ask every report buyer one feedback question by email.
- Improve the homepage using actual objections users mention.

## Best early metrics

- Assessment start rate.
- Assessment completion rate.
- Email capture rate.
- Checkout click rate.
- Report purchase rate.
- Return visits to `/app.html`.
- Focus-room sessions completed.
- Community posts/replies.

## Highest-leverage experiments

1. Report preview above checkout.
2. “6 minutes to your profile” progress framing.
3. Free email result summary before paid report upsell.
4. Founder-free branding: keep the team attribution and educational framing.
5. Separate landing pages for adult ADHD, students, workplace adjustments, late diagnosis and emotional regulation.

## Avoid for now

- Paid ads before conversion data is strong.
- Expensive custom app development.
- Complex clinician portal before consumer flow is validated.
- Rebuilding the stack before revenue justifies it.
