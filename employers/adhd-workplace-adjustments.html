Practical ADHD workplace adjustments

Practical ADHD workplace adjustments for employers, including written priorities, focus protection, clearer deadlines, predictable check-ins and reduced task switching.

Practical principle: support should be based on the employee’s actual barriers and role, not assumptions about what “an ADHD employee” needs.

Start with the barrier

An adjustment is most useful when it addresses a specific work barrier. For one person that may be interruption; for another it may be vague priorities, noisy space, memory load or difficulty switching between tasks.

Written priorities

Confirm key actions and deadlines in writing, especially after verbal discussions.

Protected focus blocks

Reduce unnecessary meetings and notifications during work that requires sustained concentration.

Milestones

Break long projects into interim deliverables instead of relying only on a distant final date.

Environmental options

Where the role allows, provide quieter locations, headphones, remote-focus blocks or flexible seating.

Do not create a universal ADHD package

Adjustments should be individualised, proportionate and compatible with the essential requirements of the role. The employee’s own account of what helps is central.

Discuss an ADHD-aware workplace programme

ADHDclearfocus offers employer education, manager training and practical workplace-support programmes. The service is organisational and educational, not staff diagnosis.
