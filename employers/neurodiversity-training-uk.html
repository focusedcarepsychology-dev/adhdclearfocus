Neurodiversity and ADHD workplace training across the UK

ADHD and neurodiversity workplace training for UK employers, with practical manager education, workflow supports and clear clinical boundaries.

Practical principle: support should be based on the employee’s actual barriers and role, not assumptions about what “an ADHD employee” needs.

Practical education for managers and teams

Our UK employer pathway focuses on the daily systems that shape whether employees can do their best work: priority clarity, interruption, communication, task sequencing, deadlines and feedback.

Training can cover

  • ADHD fundamentals without stereotypes.
  • Executive-function challenges in real work settings.
  • Manager communication and structured check-ins.
  • Examples of workplace adjustments and inclusive workflow design.
  • Boundaries between management support and clinical assessment.

UK context

Workplace support may engage duties under the Equality Act 2010 depending on individual circumstances. ADHDclearfocus provides education rather than legal advice; employers should use appropriate HR, occupational-health and legal processes for individual cases.
