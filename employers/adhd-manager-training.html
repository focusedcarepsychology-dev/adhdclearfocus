ADHD manager training that changes day-to-day management

ADHD manager training for Ireland and the UK focused on communication, priorities, feedback, task design and reasonable workplace support.

Practical principle: support should be based on the employee’s actual barriers and role, not assumptions about what “an ADHD employee” needs.

Managers need operational tools, not labels

The most useful manager training focuses on observable barriers and workable changes. A manager does not need to become an ADHD expert; they need to know how to structure work clearly and how to have a respectful support conversation.

Core topics

Priority clarity

How to distinguish “important” from “urgent” and avoid giving five simultaneous top priorities.

Feedback

How to make feedback specific, timely and behaviour-focused without unnecessary ambiguity.

Meetings and follow-up

Use concise agendas, written actions and named ownership after meetings.

Performance and support

Separate essential role outcomes from one preferred way of organising the work.

Training boundary

Managers should not diagnose employees or infer disability status. Individual support decisions should follow appropriate HR, occupational-health and legal processes.
