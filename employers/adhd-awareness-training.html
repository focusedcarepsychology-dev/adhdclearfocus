ADHD awareness training for workplaces

Evidence-informed ADHD awareness training for workplaces in Ireland and the UK, helping teams understand ADHD without stereotypes or diagnostic overreach.

Practical principle: support should be based on the employee’s actual barriers and role, not assumptions about what “an ADHD employee” needs.

What effective awareness training should achieve

Useful ADHD education moves beyond a symptom checklist. Managers need to understand how attention, working memory, task initiation, time perception, emotional regulation and environmental demands interact with real jobs.

Recognise barriers

Understand why unclear priorities, constant interruption and ambiguous deadlines can create disproportionate difficulty.

Avoid stereotypes

ADHD does not look the same in every person. Training should not encourage managers to diagnose staff.

Use practical supports

Translate awareness into clearer communication, structured priorities and better-designed workflows.

Know the boundary

Workplace education is not clinical assessment, treatment or individual medical advice.

Who it is for

HR teams, people managers, occupational-health partners and leadership groups seeking a practical introduction to ADHD and neurodiversity at work.

Discuss an ADHD-aware workplace programme

ADHDclearfocus offers employer education, manager training and practical workplace-support programmes. The service is organisational and educational, not staff diagnosis.
