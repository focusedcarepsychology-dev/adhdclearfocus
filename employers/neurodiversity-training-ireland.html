Neurodiversity and ADHD workplace training in Ireland

ADHD and neurodiversity workplace training for Irish employers, with manager education and practical process improvements designed for real teams.

Practical principle: support should be based on the employee’s actual barriers and role, not assumptions about what “an ADHD employee” needs.

Training designed for Irish workplaces

ADHDclearfocus provides organisational education for employers that want to improve inclusion without turning managers into clinicians. Programmes can focus on ADHD awareness, manager skills, communication, workflow design and practical support.

Typical outcomes

  • A shared language for discussing neurodiversity respectfully.
  • Clearer manager understanding of executive-function barriers.
  • Practical examples of low-cost process adjustments.
  • Better separation between workplace support and clinical diagnosis.

For HR and leadership

Programmes can be adapted to organisational size and role mix. For specific legal obligations under Irish employment and equality law, employers should obtain appropriate legal or HR advice.
