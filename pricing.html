Is this a diagnosis?

No. ADHDclearfocus is educational and self-awareness oriented. It does not diagnose ADHD and does not replace a formal assessment.

Why keep so much free?

Because people exploring ADHD often need immediate support before they can afford anything. The paid report funds continued development.

Can this stay low-cost?

Yes. The current architecture avoids expensive frameworks and keeps hosting lightweight on Vercel with serverless Python functions.

What comes next?

A low-cost Pro plan can add weekly reviews, saved insights, advanced tracking and premium focus rooms without blocking core support.
