About ADHDclearfocus

Learn who operates ADHDclearfocus, what the platform is designed to do, and how its educational ADHD content is separated from clinical assessment.

ADHDclearfocus is operated by Focused Care Psychology Limited. The platform provides educational ADHD self-awareness, practical tools and research summaries. It does not diagnose ADHD and using the site does not create a clinical relationship.

Why the platform exists

ADHD information online often falls into two extremes: dense clinical material that is difficult to use, or simplified social-media content that loses important nuance. ADHDclearfocus is designed to sit between those extremes — practical enough to use today, but transparent about evidence, uncertainty and clinical boundaries.

What we publish

Educational self-awareness

A structured 39-question profile that helps users organise their experiences across ten ADHD-related areas.

Evidence-led guides

Plain-English explanations of ADHD research, with limitations and source information rather than miracle claims.

Practical tools

Timers, focus supports, coping strategies and organisational tools intended to reduce friction in everyday life.

Employer education

ADHD-aware workplace training and practical neurodiversity support for organisations in Ireland and the UK.

Clinical services are separate

Where the site links to Focused Care Psychology for formal assessment or psychological services, that relationship is disclosed. ADHDclearfocus itself remains an educational platform.
