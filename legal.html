Legal, privacy and safety

Clear boundaries build trust.

ADHDclearfocus is an educational self-awareness and productivity platform. It is not a clinical service and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, crisis monitoring or emergency care.

Educational disclaimer

ADHDclearfocus provides educational information and self-awareness tools only. The screener and report do not diagnose ADHD or any other condition. Using this website does not create a clinician-client relationship. For diagnosis, treatment, medication, risk concerns or individual advice, contact an appropriately qualified professional.

Crisis and risk

This website is not monitored in real time. If you are at immediate risk, call emergency services. In Ireland and the UK, Samaritans can be contacted free on 116 123, 24/7. The Crisis Mode page is a grounding support tool, not emergency care.

Data we process

  • Assessment responses entered on the site, used to show results and optionally generate a paid report.
  • Email address if you request a report, join updates, create an account, or post in the community.
  • Local progress data stored in your browser, and account sync data if you create an account.
  • Payment data is handled by Stripe, not stored by ADHDclearfocus.
  • Email delivery is handled by SendGrid. Newsletter/list capture may be handled by Mailchimp.
  • Analytics may be collected through GA4 and Vercel Analytics to understand site performance and usage.

Community

The community is peer support, not clinical advice. Posts may be public to other users. Do not share private clinical, legal, financial, or identifying details you would not want others to see. The platform may remove harmful, abusive, spam, or unsafe content.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser local storage for progress, streaks, diary entries, account tokens and preferences such as low-stimulation mode. Analytics and third-party services may set cookies or similar identifiers. You can clear your browser storage at any time.

Contact

For privacy, support, deletion or legal queries, email focusedcarepsychology@gmail.com. For clinical concerns raised by your results, use the support links shown after the screener or contact a qualified professional.
