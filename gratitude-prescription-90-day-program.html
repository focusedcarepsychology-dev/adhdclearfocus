A practice designed to be repeatable

The programme breaks gratitude practice into short daily actions across 90 days. The emphasis is on consistency and reflection: noticing specific experiences, recording them briefly, and returning to the practice without requiring a perfect streak.

  • 90 days of structured prompts and reflection.
  • Short exercises intended to fit into everyday routines.
  • A practical format for readers who find long journaling habits difficult to sustain.
  • A wellbeing resource that can sit alongside, not replace, appropriate professional care.

Why the full title matters

This programme is distinct from other books that use the words The Gratitude Prescription. Look for the subtitle A Doctor’s 90 Day Program to Rewire Your Brain for Joy (From just 30 Seconds a Day) and Amazon identifier B0GPBYKYYL.

Start with the smallest useful action

If a wellbeing practice only works when motivation is high, it is difficult to sustain. The programme therefore starts small and builds repetition over time rather than relying on intensity at the beginning.
