Clear about the evidence

Helpful information. Honest limitations.

ADHDclearfocus is an educational platform operated by Focused Care Psychology Limited. We aim to make ADHD research easier to understand and everyday strategies easier to try.

The 39-question self-reflection is not a validated diagnostic assessment. We explain how it works, distinguish educational guidance from treatment and link to sources so you can make informed choices.
