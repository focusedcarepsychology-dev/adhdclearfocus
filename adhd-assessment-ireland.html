ADHD assessment in Ireland: what a proper assessment looks for

A formal ADHD assessment is broader than an online test. It looks at developmental history, current symptoms, impairment, other possible explanations and the person’s wider mental-health context.

Short answer: In Ireland, screening questionnaires can support an ADHD assessment, but they are not sufficient on their own. The HSE adult ADHD Model of Care describes a process that includes screening, ADHD assessment, identification of co-occurring conditions, diagnosis and a post-assessment discussion.

What the Irish public model says

The HSE Adult ADHD Model of Care describes adult assessment as a multi-stage process. It highlights developmental history, current functional impairment and possible co-existing mental-health conditions rather than relying on a single score.

Why screening is only one part

The HSE model includes self-rating scales in pre-assessment screening, including the WHO Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale. A screening result helps decide what should be explored further; it does not itself establish ADHD.

What information is useful to gather

Childhood evidence

School reports, old comments, family recollections and examples of early attention, organisation or activity difficulties can all help build developmental context.

Current impairment

Bring concrete examples from work, study, relationships, driving, finances, household management and daily organisation.

Other conditions

Sleep problems, anxiety, depression, autism, trauma, substance use and physical-health issues may overlap with or coexist alongside ADHD.

Existing treatment

Bring current medication lists, relevant reports and details of previous psychological or psychiatric care where applicable.

Public and private pathways are not identical

The HSE Model of Care describes the public adult ADHD pathway, including referral through mental-health services. Private assessment routes can differ in referral process, waiting time and follow-up arrangements. Before booking privately, ask exactly what the assessment includes and what happens after diagnosis if medication or further treatment is needed.
